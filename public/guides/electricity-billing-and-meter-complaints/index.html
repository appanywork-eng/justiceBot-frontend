Power and electricity · Nigerian complaint guidance

Electricity complaints: estimated bills, meters, disconnections and supply

Prepare a useful complaint about an electricity distribution company, disputed meter reading, estimated bill, disconnection or supply problem in Nigeria.

By PetitionDesk Editorial Team · Reviewed 25 August 2026

Who this guide is for: Residential or business electricity customers challenging a bill, connection decision, meter issue or unresolved service interruption.

Understand the problem before writing

A power complaint becomes easier to investigate when it identifies the electricity distribution company, the service address and the relevant billing period. A general statement that electricity supply is poor rarely shows whether the problem concerns a disputed bill, a damaged meter, a service connection, a prolonged outage or a disconnection decision.

For billing disputes, compare the account number, customer classification, recorded meter reading, amount demanded and previous bills. If the meter stopped working or the bill was estimated, explain the date the problem was reported and preserve any technician visit record. Do not assume every high bill is unlawful; identify the specific charge or reading you want reviewed.

Where the complaint involves disconnection, describe the notice received, the date service stopped, any disputed arrears and the effect on the household or business. Separate an emergency safety issue, such as exposed wiring or a damaged transformer, from an ordinary billing disagreement and report an immediate hazard through the relevant emergency channel first.

Electricity oversight may depend on the distribution area and whether responsibility sits with the national regulator, a recognised consumer forum or a state electricity authority. The location supplied in the complaint therefore matters as much as the provider name.

Build an evidence checklist

Use records that relate directly to your complaint. Put events in date order, identify the organisation involved, and keep copies of every message or acknowledgement. Describe what your documents actually show; do not present an allegation as a proven finding.

  • Distribution company and electricity account or meter number
  • Service address and state
  • Disputed bills, receipts and meter readings
  • Dates of outages, visits or disconnection
  • The provider complaint reference and response
  • Photographs or maintenance records where safely available

Choose the institution that can respond

Start with the electricity distribution company serving the affected address. Use its official customer-care or complaint channel, request a reference and identify the billing or connection decision being challenged. An existing unresolved provider complaint may support referral to the applicable electricity consumer forum or regulator.

A company mentioned in a statement, address, receipt or background document is not automatically the institution responsible for the complaint. Identify who provided the disputed service, who made the decision, and who can supply records or correct the problem.

Decide whether escalation is justified

When the provider does not resolve the matter, submit the earlier complaint, service address, account details, disputed bills and the remedy requested to the authority with jurisdiction over that electricity market. Confirm whether a national forum or state-level regulator is responsible before sending the petition.

Before escalating, keep the original complaint reference, the institution's written answer where available, and a concise explanation of what remains unresolved. Check the current instructions published by the relevant authority instead of assuming that one deadline applies to every type of complaint.

Worked example: applying the route in practice

A customer in Abuja disputing several months of estimated charges should identify the distribution company, attach the affected bills, state whether a functioning meter exists and request a documented meter inspection, billing reconciliation and written outcome.

Avoid common mistakes and protect personal data

Do not quote a tariff, reconnection fee, compensation amount or statutory timeline unless it comes from a verified current source that applies to the customer category and location.

Remove passwords, one-time passcodes, card security codes and unrelated medical, financial or family information. Request a specific remedy supported by your evidence, such as an explanation, correction, investigation, refund or written decision.

Frequently asked questions

Can I bypass the electricity company immediately?

Usually begin with the provider unless there is an immediate danger, a documented unresolved complaint or another established route requiring direct contact with the regulator.

Does my location matter?

Yes. The service address helps determine the distribution company, the correct forum and whether a relevant state electricity authority has jurisdiction.
